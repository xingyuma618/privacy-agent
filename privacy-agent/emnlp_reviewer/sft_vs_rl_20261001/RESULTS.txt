============================================================

----------------

infrastructure errors. The strict report is results_local_qwen/summary.json

Interpretation limits
---------------------

This is a new local-Qwen-backend supplementary comparison. The RL artifact is
the verified global_step_10 intermediate checkpoint stored under the
historically misleading checkpoint-30 directory; it is not established as the
paper-final or converged RL model. The local results must not be compared as
absolute reproduction values against the paper's cloud-backed evaluation.

Concise rebuttal wording
------------------------

“We added a controlled supplementary comparison between the verified SFT
parent and the surviving RL global-step-10 intermediate checkpoint, using the
same 300 cases and local Qwen SQL-generation backend over three inference
repetitions. The RL checkpoint did not improve execution accuracy in this
setting (RL-SFT: -1.67 percentage points; paired question-cluster bootstrap
95% CI [-3.22, -0.11]) and showed no detectable change in the reported surface
privacy metrics. We therefore do not present this intermediate checkpoint as
the paper-final RL model or as a reproduction of the paper's cloud-backed
absolute score.”
